Your fortnight
Your rules

  • No training through pain or exhaustion.

Simple skincare routine

Morning: gentle cleanse or rinse → moisturiser → broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen.

Evening: gentle cleanser → moisturiser.

For these two weeks, don't buy lots of products or introduce several new active ingredients at once. A simple routine is a good start, especially when you're unsure of your skin type.

If breakouts or irritation persist, ask a parent or guardian about getting advice from a healthcare professional.

Your October 24 goal

You don't need to reach a particular weight, appearance or workout milestone. Aim to have discovered:

  • A skincare routine that feels easy.
  • A few personal style ideas you genuinely love.
  • A beginner strength routine that feels manageable.
  • Ways to eat a varied, satisfying range of foods during school.
  • An evening routine that helps you get enough rest.
  • A little more space for creativity, fun and self-expression.

The real glow-up is a life where looking after yourself feels natural, not like another assignment. Keep the habits that help you feel well and let go of the ones that feel like pressure.
